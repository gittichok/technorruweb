## Turn what you love into a career

If you wonder how machines work, love taking things apart, fixing and building them, or sketching ideas until they become real objects, that curiosity could be the start of the right career for you.

The Faculty of Industrial Technology is in Chachoengsao, part of Thailand's Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC), one of the country's most important industrial hubs. We aim to lead the East in hands-on technology and engineering, producing graduates who can do the work and grow with industry at home.

## Seven programmes to choose from

- **Engineering (B.Eng., 4 years):** Automated Manufacturing, Electrical and Energy, Modern Automotive, Industrial Management, and Logistics and Process Engineering
- **Technology (B.Tech. continuing, 2 years):** Smart Industrial Technology, for vocational graduates moving into smart factories
- **Design (B.Sc., 4 years):** Creative Design Innovation, for those who want to turn ideas into products

## Learn by doing, with industry

Our programmes are outcome-based and built with industry partners from day one. Through cooperative education and Cooperative and Work Integrated Education (CWIE), you work in real factories and organisations while still a student.

## Automation, data and AI

Today's factories run on automation, digital technology and data. Our programmes build foundations in smart industrial technology and use AI in production, design and learning, always checking accuracy, using it responsibly and considering its impact on people and the environment.

## Knowledge that gives back

We educate for local development. You will apply knowledge and innovation to real community problems, improving products and processes while using resources wisely.

## More than an employee

Our graduates work as engineers, public servants, researchers and designers. Many programmes also nurture entrepreneurship, from electrical contracting to logistics and creative businesses.

## Character matters

We want graduates who are eager to learn and ready to work, with integrity and a spirit of service, and who practise communication, teamwork, leadership and professional ethics along the way.
