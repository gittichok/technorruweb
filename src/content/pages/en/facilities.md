The faculty teaches on the main campus of Rajabhat Rajanagarindra University in Chachoengsao and at the Bang Khla education centre.

## Bang Khla laboratory building

A four-storey laboratory building funded with 80 million baht of government budget was completed in March 2016 and houses laboratories for every programme.
